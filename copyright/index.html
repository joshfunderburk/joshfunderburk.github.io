Copyright and DMCA Takedown Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

Our approach

FunderBook respects copyright and responds to notices of alleged infringement under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512. FunderBook does not host user-uploaded files or public user posts: your reading records and notes are private to your account. The material most likely to raise a copyright concern is book cover images and descriptions, which come from our catalog provider and are shown in the app.

How to send a takedown notice

Email joshfunderburk91@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright notice". The DMCA asks that a notice include: (1) your physical or electronic signature; (2) a description of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed; (3) a description of where the material appears in FunderBook, such as the book and edition, with enough detail for us to find it; (4) your name, mailing address, telephone number and email address; (5) a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and (6) a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for the owner.

Notices that leave out these details may not be actionable, and we may ask you for more information. Knowingly sending a false notice can make you liable for damages.

What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we act promptly to remove or disable access to the identified material. Cover images and descriptions are supplied by our catalog provider, so we may also pass your notice to them and ask them to remove the material at the source.

Counter-notices

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, email joshfunderburk91@gmail.com with the subject "Counter-notice". Include: (1) your physical or electronic signature; (2) a description of the material removed and where it appeared before removal; (3) a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; and (4) your name, address and telephone number, a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, if outside the United States, for any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We will send a copy to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the material.

Repeat infringers

We will close the accounts of users who are repeat infringers, in appropriate circumstances.

Other concerns

For concerns that are not copyright, such as privacy requests, see the Privacy Policy or email joshfunderburk91@gmail.com. This policy is not legal advice. If you are unsure whether material infringes your rights, consider speaking to a lawyer before sending a notice.
